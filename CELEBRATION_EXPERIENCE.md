## 💫 Confetti Physics

```
Particle Properties:
- Count: ~50 particles per burst
- Bursts: 2 simultaneous (left & right sides)
- Velocity: 30 units/s
- Spread: 360° (all directions)
- Gravity: Yes (particles fall)
- Colors: Random (red, blue, green, yellow, purple, pink)
- Duration: 3 seconds
- Z-index: 9999 (above everything)

Burst Locations:
Left Burst:  x: 10-30% from left, y: random
Right Burst: x: 70-90% from left, y: random

Effect:
```
